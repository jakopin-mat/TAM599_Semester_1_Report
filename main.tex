For silicon in the 8-atom conventional cell, total energy is converged to within 1 meV/atom at ENCUT = 400 eV (reference: 600 eV) and a 5 × 5 × 5 Gamma-centered mesh (reference: 13 × 13 × 13). Evidence: `encutconvergence.png`, `kpointsconvergence.png`. Energy differences between cells of the same size converge by 250 eV. Cell relaxation at 250 eV gives a lattice constant 0.0036 Å smaller than at 550 eV, consistent with Pulay stress.
